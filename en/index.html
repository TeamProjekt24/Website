The Project

We are three successful sim racers with a clear vision: to set a historic milestone in motorsport. As pioneers of innovative talent development, we show how the leap from virtual racing to the real track can be made – directly and without detours.

Our team consists of Lucas Müller, Benedikt Hitz and Jonas Wanner – three professional esports athletes who have been competing at the highest level in sim racing for years. Our achievements include the ADAC DTM Esports Championship 2025, the ADAC Simracing Cup GT4 Championship 2025 and the DNLS CC SP9 & SP10 Championship 2025.

Our goal: to write motorsport history – as the first of our kind at the Ring. The path leads via RCN 2026 and NLS 2026/27 to the 24h Nürburgring 2027. Passion, precision and 24 hours flat out.

PROJEKT 24 combines talent, technology and team spirit in a unique way. A pioneering project with an authentic success story that inspires a new generation for traditional motorsport and demonstrates the bridge between the virtual and real racing worlds.
